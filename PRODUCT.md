# Product context

## Who it is for

Minti English serves grade-5 students (10–11 years old) in Hanoi preparing for English entrance exams into high-quality grade-6 schools, plus their parents.

## What makes it different

Generic English apps teach broad skills. Minti targets the actual exam scope: Grammar, Vocabulary, and 11 recurring question types used by the target schools. It deliberately excludes IELTS, TOEIC, Listening, Speaking, and essay Writing in v1.

The product combines four pillars into one closed loop:

1. **Exam library** — school-specific papers and question sets.
2. **Mock-test engine** — timed, exam-shaped practice.
3. **Assessment and error analysis** — what the student missed, why, and what to practice next.
4. **Gamified motivation** — character growth, streaks, rewards, and visible progress.

The student loop is: placement → practice weak areas → take a school-specific mock or past paper → review mistakes → return to an updated progress view.

## Product principles

- Exam-specific beats generic English.
- Show the next useful action; do not make a 10-year-old choose from a blank slate.
- Make progress visible, not stressful.
- Earn parent trust through specificity: target school, question-type analysis, gap-to-target, source transparency.
- Do not fake content or intelligence. If the data is missing, show a calm empty state.

## Current status

- v1.0 milestone completed.
- 9 golden-path student journeys covered by Playwright E2E tests.
- Next.js + TypeScript + Prisma, with auth, middleware, content features, and a Prisma schema.
- Private codebase; public pitch and walkthrough available on request.

## Why content is the moat

The UI can be copied. A validated, school-tagged question bank cannot. Every additional paper improves placement accuracy, error analysis, mock coverage, and parent trust. That is the compounding asset the bot is built to produce.
